Privacy Policy

Application: PN Junction Developer / Publisher: Naren Kishore G D Effective Date: October 18, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how PN Junction ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), authored and published by Naren Kishore G D, collects, processes, uses, and safeguards information when you use our Windows Desktop Application (distributed via Microsoft Store and standalone distribution) and our web application.

1. Overview and Core Privacy Principles

PN Junction is an AI-assisted engineering workspace built for hardware and software developers to inspect 3D geometries, electronic schematics, PCB layouts, and collaborate on engineering projects.

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or engineering data. Data is collected solely to provide workspace features, user authentication, and real-time team collaboration.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with PN Junction, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in, we process your email address, display name, user handle (ID), and password (handled securely via Google Firebase Authentication). If you choose to sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your verified Google email and public profile name.
  • User Profile Information: Any details you voluntarily provide in your profile settings, such as your bio, public GitHub repository link, and custom profile picture (DP).
  • Direct Messages & Team Communications: Text messages, reactions, and file attachments sent between users in collaborative chats.
  • Engineering Projects: Project names, member access lists, and project files (such as CAD files, schematics, PCB layers, or documentation) uploaded to shared project workspaces.
  • Google Drive Integration (Optional): If you connect Google Drive to sync your files, we process your OAuth authorization token strictly to view and load your selected Drive files. We do not store or inspect your private Google Drive files on our servers.
Local File Inspection Privacy: When you open a local file from your PC (e.g., Blender .blend, KiCad PCB .kicad_pcb, Gerber, STL, OBJ), rendering and inspection take place 100% locally on your device using local CLI tools and WebGL. Local files are never uploaded to the cloud unless you explicitly attach them to a chat or upload them to a shared project.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for the following legitimate operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and secure access to your workspace.
  • To deliver real-time messaging, file exchange, and project collaboration features with teammates.
  • To store and render your profile picture (DP) and verification badges across conversation and project headers.
  • To resolve user lookup queries when you search for a teammate by unique user ID or handle.
  • To notify you about incoming direct messages or project updates.

4. Third-Party Services and Subprocessors

PN Junction utilizes trusted, enterprise-grade cloud services to deliver secure infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase (Alphabet Inc.): Used for user authentication, cloud database storage (Firestore), and media storage. Firebase complies with major data privacy regulations (ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3). Privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Google Identity & Drive API: Used strictly for optional user-authorized sign-in and Google Drive file synchronization. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Microsoft Store Services: For Windows Desktop Store editions, installation, packaging, and automatic differential updates are handled directly by Microsoft Corporation under the Microsoft Privacy Statement: privacy.microsoft.com.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We employ rigorous administrative and technical security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmissions between the application and cloud endpoints are encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL (HTTPS).
  • Authentication tokens and passwords are never stored in plaintext.
  • Granular Firestore Security Rules ensure that private chats and project files are accessible only to authorized members.
  • We retain your account data and project files for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide workspace collaboration.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal information and uploaded content:

  • Delete Uploaded Content: You can delete projects, uploaded files, and messages directly within the application at any time.
  • Profile Editing: You can edit or remove your display name, bio, GitHub link, and profile photo directly in Settings.
  • Account & Data Erasure: To request complete deletion of your account and all associated Firestore records, send a request to narenkishore85@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

PN Junction is an engineering and developer workspace tool. It is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in the application or legal requirements. When updates are published, the "Effective Date" at the top of this document will be revised accordingly. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
